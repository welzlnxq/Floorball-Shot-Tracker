Floorball Shot Tracker

Instructions for observer:
  1. Input the 3 attacking players' names.
  2. Observe the gameplay and the attacking team's advancement towards the goal.
  3. When a shot is made by any of the attacking players:
    1. note the result
    2. select the corresponding button
    3. indicate on the court roughly where the shot was made
  4. If possession is lost by the attacking team before a shot was made, indicate it on the court.

Tap an existing counter to remove it.
